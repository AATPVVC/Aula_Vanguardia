School Safety Proposals: Solutions

1. Access Control

Installation of metal detectors at the entrance to prevent weapons from being brought into the premises.

2. Communication and Tracking App

Enables parents and guardians to monitor students' locations, track school attendance, and receive reports of fights.

3. Risk Mapping

Includes a section in the app with a map of high-risk areas where conflicts occur most frequently.

4. Automatic Detection

Instantly notifies teachers and school staff when the cameras detect violent incidents.

5. Biometric Panic Buttons

Enable rapid alerts activated by fingerprint authentication to record the time of the incident and identify the person reporting the fight.

6. Panic Button Monitoring

Dedicated cameras installed above the panic buttons to verify the authenticity of each reported incident.
