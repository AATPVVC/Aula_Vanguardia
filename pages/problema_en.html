Problems

Physical Violence

Although less common than verbal abuse, it is the most visible and most frequently reported due to the immediate risk it poses to students’ safety. As with all forms of violence, the aggressor’s primary goal is not only—or not always—to cause physical pain, but also to limit the other person’s self-determination.

Verbal Abuse

Verbal abuse is a form of interpersonal violence characterized by the use of harmful language to exert power and control over victims. It manifests itself through intimidating comments, harassment, and manipulative tactics intended to shame, humiliate, or threaten people, which often causes profound emotional distress.

Psychological/Emotional (Social) Abuse

It almost always goes hand in hand with verbal abuse and tends to be less visible, but it has a profound impact on victims. It involves undermining a person’s self-esteem through constant criticism, belittling their abilities, insults, and other forms of verbal abuse.
