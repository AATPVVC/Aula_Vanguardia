Propuestas de Seguridad Escolar: Soluciones

1. Access Control:

Installation of metal detectors at the entrance to prevent weapons from being brought in.

2. Communication and Tracking App:

Allows parents to monitor students’ locations and school attendance, and to receive reports of fights.

3. Risk Mapping:

Includes a section in the app with a map of “red zones” where conflicts occur most frequently.

4. Automatic detection:

Instant notification to teachers and supervisors when cameras detect violence.

5. Biometric panic buttons:

Quick alerts activated by fingerprint to record the time and the person reporting the fight.

6. Button verification:

Dedicated cameras above the panic buttons to verify the authenticity of the report.
